### Accessibility notes

- Never encode meaning in colour alone. Q1 uses word plus icon, and the star’s colour is a result, not an input.
- Use large tap targets, one question per screen, and a progress indicator.
- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` in the web app, and keep the rotation range wide enough to read at a distance in the installation.
- State “no wrong answers” once at the start, in warm, plain language.

1 Very quiet · 2 Mostly quiet · 3 In between · 4 Quite busy · 5 Racing


# Open ended prompt

## Main 
If someone else in the library was feeling the exact same way, what would you want them to hear? 
	You can share a thought, a piece of advice, reassurance, or something you wish someone told you today.

## Alternative pool:
- If someone else felt the way you do right now, what would you want them to hear?
- What would you like someone nearby to know?
- If you could share some words of encouragement with someone else in the library today, what would it be?
- What is one quiet thought you've been carrying with you today as you study?
- What does a moment of peace feel like to you in the middle of a busy university week?
- If this room could hear one gentle wish for everyone sharing this space right now, what would you say?
- What is something that helped ground you recently? It could be a sound, a sight, a memory or even a cup of tea!
- What is you happy place?
