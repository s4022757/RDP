# Overview
You will deliver a 15-minute presentation in class, a slide presentation of up to 20 slides, and the following design response.

Your **presentation** will include:
- a prototype or demonstration of your project that you will talk through the key elements of production and how it responds to the Libraries Against Loneliness Brief
- an explanation of your work process through the semester - who did what, what did you find out, how did you create your prototype, why you chose the methods you used
- a critical reflection by your group on the obstacles and surprises you discovered building these creative responses

Depending on your practice you can submit a conceptual design response as either:
- An initial video prototype as 3840 x 2160, 25 fps, H264, Mp4, 
- A stereo or multichannel audio
- For a work made in unity, submit at minimum a **Mac OSX build** and a **screen recording** of the work functioning. You can also submit a PC build and a HTML build for the sake of accessibility.
- A web-based work, at minimum, submit **a link to the work online**, and a **screen recording** of it functioning.
# Brief
In this assessment task, your group will present a fully realised design in the delivery of the briefed Libraries Against Loneliness project in class by a ten-minute, up to 20 slide presentation of your design concept. 

Your presentation will aim to present your group’s design as a site response to the Libraries Against Loneliness Brief. You will present the final design as part of this discussion. You will identify the strengths and failings of the workflows used using design thinking as a foundation methodology. You will also present your timeline, risk assessment, production workflow and budget.

You will demonstrate in this assignment:

- Incorporation of feedback from assignment 2 presentations and submission
- A cohesive and creative response to the provided brief
- A clear understanding of the conceptual parameters of context and constraint within the chosen site.
- Evidence of responding to audience, stakeholder and public engagement
- Evidence of consideration of accessible design principles
- Evidence of site research
- Demonstrated identification of site constraints
- A clear understanding of elements required to realise this concept
- Documentation of proposed production workflow, timeline, risk assessment and budget
- A resolved methodology in support of your concept response

You'll need in the assignment to identify which team member did what task and those tasks require identifying how many hours each to determine a pass in the assignment.

To do this, you'll need to keep a log book gantt chart spreadsheet which is available as a downloadable template in modules and provide a link to this in your assignment.

# Outcomes
## Course Outcomes

- Apply digital media production processes, individually and in teams, to resolve design problems
- Investigate and select appropriate production processes for site responsive project delivery

## Program Learning Outcome

- Demonstrate creativity, critical thinking and innovation when identifying and solving problems in diverse contexts within the discipline.
- Apply theoretical knowledge, conceptual skills and production techniques to the development of digital media solutions for design problems.

- Work with others in a range of roles and contexts, demonstrating cultural, environmental and social awareness and ethical and reflective practice.

# Assessment Criteria:  
The assignment will be graded on five individual criteria:

1. Realisation of a creative and original concept 20%: Developing and articulating an intriguing concept and justifying this through experimentation, conceptual rationalisation and design choices with particular attention to responding to the supplied brief.   
2. Technical proficiency 20%: Showing proficiency in specialist skills regarding your brief.  
3. Unified application of the elements and principles of design 20%: Handling of a sense of logic or style that is applied overall to your concept, whether it be visually or through experience and sound design.   
4. Professional presentation 20%: Including well-designed presentation, logical progression of ideas and ability to speak and justify design choices.  
5. Work Record 20%: Regarding evidence of ongoing professional commitment to set tasks, both individually and in a group. You will also be expected as part of this to critically engage with your classmate's presentations.
